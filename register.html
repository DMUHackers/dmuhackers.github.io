How to Register

Follow these steps to get signed up for Pwn2Play: Core Incursion on Biterra.

Scroll
1

Create a Biterra Account

Head to biterra.co and register for a free account. This is the platform that hosts all Pwn2Play challenges.

Go to Biterra
2

Join the Pwn2Play Event

Once logged in, navigate to the Pwn2Play: Core Incursion event and join it. This gives you access to the competition space.

Pwn2Play on Biterra
Choose your path

Team Captain

Create a team and recruit members

3

Create Your Team

Within the Pwn2Play event, create a new team. Choose a team name and set it up ready for your members.

4

Share the Invite Link

Copy your team's invite link and share it with your teammates. They'll use this to join your team directly.

Team Member

Join an existing team

3

Get the Invite Link

Ask your team captain for the invite link. They'll have this ready once they've created the team.

4

Join the Team

Click the invite link to join your captain's team. You'll be added to the roster and ready to compete.

You're all set
5

Save the Date

Pwn2Play: Core Incursion takes place Saturday 30th May 2026, 09:00–18:00. Add it to your calendar so you don't miss it.
