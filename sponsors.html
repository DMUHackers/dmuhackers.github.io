Get Involved

What We're Looking For

There are several ways your organisation can get involved with Pwn2Play and DMU Hackers. In return, your company will be officially affiliated with Pwn2Play.

Challenge Creators

Design and supply CTF challenges for our Pwn2Play events. Bring real-world scenarios from your domain and test our competitors with problems that matter.

Sponsors

Contribute prizes, funding or resources to help us run bigger and better events. From cash prizes to hardware, every contribution makes a difference.

Participating Teams

Enter a team from your organisation to compete alongside students. A great way to test your own talent, scout future hires and build your brand in the community.

Interested in getting involved? We'd love to hear from you.
